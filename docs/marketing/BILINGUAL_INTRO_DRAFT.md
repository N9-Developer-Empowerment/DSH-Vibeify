# DSH Vibeify bilingual introduction

Status: **draft; fluent Simplified-Chinese human review required before public posting**.

## English

### Turn your AI work into a living magazine

DSH Vibeify is an open-source, local-first magazine experience for DeepSeek Harness. It opens into useful visual content instead of an empty prompt, while Chat remains available whenever you want to request, steer or inspect the work.

Use native DeepSeek, a ChatGPT-authenticated Codex agent, or both. In combined mode, Codex can plan and verify bounded work while DeepSeek handles eligible execution. The product keeps those provider arrangements distinct and does not claim verification that did not occur.

Your magazine, preferences and editorial learning remain in your browser. Sharing is optional and article-by-article: inspect a private preview first, then separately choose whether to publish a public link. The share contract excludes prompts, reasoning, session identity, approvals, local history and credentials.

DSH Vibeify is macOS-first and DeepSeek Harness remains a developer preview. Windows and Linux installers are currently preview routes. Provider work may use the network and may have separate costs.

## 简体中文

### 把你的 AI 工作变成一本持续更新的杂志

DSH Vibeify 是一个面向 DeepSeek Harness 的开源、本地优先杂志体验。打开后，你首先看到的是有用的可视化内容，而不是一个空白提示框；当你想提出要求、调整方向或查看工作过程时，仍然可以随时进入 Chat。

你可以使用原生 DeepSeek、通过 ChatGPT 登录的 Codex 智能体，或者同时使用两者。在组合模式下，Codex 可以负责规划和核验有明确边界的工作，DeepSeek 则执行适合委派的任务。产品会清楚区分这些模式，也不会声称进行过实际上并未发生的核验。

你的杂志内容、偏好和编辑学习记录都保存在本地浏览器中。分享是可选的，并且一次只分享一篇文章：先查看私密预览，再单独决定是否发布公开链接。分享数据不包含提示词、推理过程、会话身份、审批信息、本地历史记录或凭据。

目前 DSH Vibeify 以 macOS 为主，DeepSeek Harness 本身仍处于开发者预览阶段。Windows 和 Linux 安装程序目前也标记为预览版。模型服务可能需要联网，并可能产生各自的费用。
