## Distribution contract

Every article should produce:

1. one canonical substantial version in the Vibeify repository Discussion;
2. a useful update comment on the single official DSH project Discussion, because the category permits only one discussion per project;
3. a compact Discord version with one concrete technical or product insight;
4. separate substantial posts for r/DeepSeek and r/LocalLLaMA, adapted to each community;
5. a short release-note link when the article directly explains a shipped capability;
6. one reviewed Vibe public-share page when the author chooses to dogfood sharing.

Never copy local prompts, private magazine history, reasoning, approvals or account data into a public proof. Do not claim human testing, fluent translation review, provider verification or platform compatibility without the corresponding evidence.
