# DSH Vibeify 0.15.4

## SoundCloud players now fit their content

Vibe and the private/public article reader now render SoundCloud tracks with the provider's compact 166-pixel player. The earlier generic media frame could leave hundreds of pixels of blank white space below the waveform, especially on wide screens.

The fix is provider-specific:

- SoundCloud tracks use the compact player at desktop and phone widths.
- Spotify keeps its music-player height.
- YouTube and Vimeo keep their responsive video frame.
- Playback remains click-to-load, sandboxed and non-autoplaying.
- The ordinary SoundCloud link remains visible when the embedded player is unavailable.

The release includes regression checks for both the local Vibe renderer and the separately hosted preview/public reader.
