# DSH Vibeify 0.15.5

**Your Chat-made Vibes survive relaunches, and every public article gets its own image.**

This release restores completed streamed Vibe chunks from durable DSH history and makes public imagery deliberately stricter than personal reading. A local magazine may reuse its small offline catalogue. A public share may not reuse an image that has appeared on any earlier Vibeify public page.

## What changed

- Completed streamed Chat-made Vibes can be restored from durable DSH history after a relaunch instead of depending only on the final short answer.
- Public sharing permanently records canonical image URLs, ignoring crop and resize query variants, so removing or expiring an article never makes its image reusable.
- Public visual priority is a fresh relevant photograph, then a fresh story-specific generated image, then a unique AI-assisted graphic, then a one-off typographic editorial cover.
- A text-only article—or one whose proposed images were used before—gets a unique 1200×630 JPEG made from its reviewed title and a short article excerpt. Prompts, reasoning, and transcripts are never used.
- Generated visuals are labelled and never presented as real photography.
- The private preview checks visual freshness before publication. A concurrent conflict fails safely and asks the reader to preview again rather than silently changing the article.
- The final public cover also supplies the article's Open Graph and X preview image.
- Sharing, security, architecture, and reader documentation now explain the personal/public distinction and permanent no-reuse rule.

## Upgrade and rollback

Finish active DSH work before upgrading. The friendly installer installs an immutable package, canary-boots it away from the live port, and restarts only after idle confirmation.

To roll back, download the `0.15.4` release assets, install that versioned source, then restart DSH after current work finishes. Removing Vibeify does not delete DSH sessions, Codex authentication, DeepSeek credentials, browser-stored Vibe preferences, or previously published public pages.

## Honest boundaries

A highly relevant real image still depends on a lawful public source and usable credit. Image generation depends on the installed provider and the reader authorising that generation. When neither is available, the unique typographic cover keeps the public page visual without recycling unrelated stock. Existing public pages keep their earlier images; the migration reserves them so new articles cannot repeat them.
