# Vibeify 0.16.5

The magazine now has a prominent, permanent masthead for each website look. VIBE magazine uses a large italic wordmark and its fixed strapline; the newsroom look uses boxed VIBE lettering and a NEWS band. The same saved identity appears in the magazine, library, private article preview and public article page.

Brand identities and responsive styles live in one shared catalogue. The saved look restores its identity across reloads and switching looks, without regenerating logos or sending additional settings in the share payload. Existing publication writing voices remain paired with their looks.

DSH compatibility remains 0.2.0-rc.2, bundled Codex 0.160.0, Visuals 0.1.1 and Social Desk 0.2.1. Windows and Linux installers remain preview platforms.
