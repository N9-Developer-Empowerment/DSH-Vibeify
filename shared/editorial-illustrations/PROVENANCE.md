# Open Doodles editorial illustrations

These locally bundled SVGs are original Open Doodles illustrations by Pablo Stanley. The [official gallery](https://www.opendoodles.com/) links to each file, and the [About page](https://www.opendoodles.com/about) grants CC0 use. Attribution is included in Vibe as a courtesy. These images are conceptual illustrations of article themes, not depictions of a reported event.
